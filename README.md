# Rehearsal

**Practise the interview before it counts.**

A mock interview that actually behaves like one: it asks a question, listens to
your answer, scores it honestly, and **digs into weak answers instead of politely
moving on** — then finishes with a full debrief telling you whether you'd pass.

👉 **Try it:** https://rahulatrkm.github.io/rehearsal/

## Why it's different

Most "AI interview" tools generate a list of questions and stop. Rehearsal is a
real conversation:

- 🎙️ **Answer out loud** — speak your answers with the mic (or type). Practising
  aloud is how you actually get better at interviews.
- 📊 **Scored every turn** — 0–10 on each answer, with what worked, what to fix,
  and a stronger version of *your own* answer using only facts you gave.
- 🔍 **It probes you** — vague answer with no numbers? It asks the uncomfortable
  follow-up, exactly like a real interviewer.
- 📋 **Full debrief** — an overall score, an honest "would this pass?" verdict,
  habits across your answers, what to drill, and the questions to prepare next.

## Tailored to you

Choose the **role**, **seniority** (entry → lead) and **interview type**
(behavioural, technical, mixed, or a tough pressure interview).

## Honest about what it is

An AI interviewer to sharpen your answers — **not a hiring decision** and not a
guarantee. It won't invent achievements for you; the model answers only reshape
what you actually said.

**Nothing is stored.** No signup, no account, free.

## Tech

A static page plus a small free AI backend. Voice input uses the browser's built-in
speech recognition (Chrome/Edge); typing works everywhere. No build step, no
tracking.
